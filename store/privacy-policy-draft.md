## What we collect

- **Your Google account basics:** your name, e-mail address, profile picture link and Google account ID, when you
  sign in with Google.
- **What you do with LAMISS:** the YouTube videos you use it on (video ID, title, duration), where you stopped,
  the questions LAMISS asks you and your answers, the questions you ask LAMISS and its answers, and your
  practice time.
- **Your voice, only when you use the microphone:** the recording of a spoken question or answer is sent to be
  turned into text. We keep the text, not the recording.
- **Your settings:** languages, level, goals, tutor settings, voice and username.
- **Video captions:** the captions of the YouTube videos you use LAMISS on.

Settings that only concern your device (microphone, speaker, volume, app language) stay in your browser.

## Why

Only to provide LAMISS's single purpose: to let an AI tutor practice a language with you on the video you
watch, and to keep your settings and progress. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it for
anything unrelated to that purpose.
