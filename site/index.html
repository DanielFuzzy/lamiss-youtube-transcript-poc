LAMISS

Your AI language tutor on YouTube

LAMISS is a Chrome extension that practices a language with you while you watch YouTube: it asks you questions about the video, answers yours by voice or text, and keeps your progress.

Getting started

  1. Click the LAMISS icon in the Chrome toolbar, read the privacy notice and choose Agree and sign in with Google.
  2. Open a YouTube video that has captions and press Start Learning (or the LAMISS button under the video).
  3. Answer LAMISS's questions out loud or by typing, and ask your own in the chat next to the video.

Common questions

LAMISS says the video has no captions.
LAMISS reads the video's captions. Pick a video with captions (the CC button in the player).
The microphone doesn't work.
In the popup, open Audio & Voice, choose Allow microphone, then use Test microphone.
How do I change the language of LAMISS or of the tutor?
The flags at the top of the popup change the extension's language. Tutor Behavior → Tutor language changes the language the tutor speaks.
How do I delete my data?
Profile & Account → Delete my account. See the privacy policy.
