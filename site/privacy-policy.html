LAMISS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

LAMISS is a Chrome extension that lets an AI tutor practice a language with you on the YouTube videos you watch. It is an independent project run by Daniel (GitHub: DanielFuzzy), who you can reach at sanchezramosdaniel@gmail.com. This policy explains what LAMISS collects, why, who else processes it, and how you can delete it.

What LAMISS collects

  • Your Google account basics: your name, e-mail address, profile picture link and Google account ID, when you sign in with Google.
  • What you do with LAMISS: the YouTube videos you use it on (video ID, title and length), where you stopped watching, the questions LAMISS asks you and your answers, the questions you ask LAMISS and its answers, the feedback on your answers, and how many minutes you practiced.
  • Your voice, only when you use the microphone: when you ask or answer out loud, the recording is sent to be turned into text. LAMISS keeps the text, not the recording.
  • Your settings: native and learning language, level, goals, tutor and voice settings, and username.
  • Video captions: the captions of the YouTube videos you use LAMISS on. They are public content, not personal data, and are shared by everyone who uses LAMISS on the same video.

Some settings stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere: your microphone and speaker choice, volume, question frequency and the language of the extension.

LAMISS only runs on www.youtube.com, and only on the video you start it on. It does not read other websites or your browsing history, and does not collect payment details, location or contacts.

Why

Only to do what LAMISS is for: let the AI tutor ask and answer questions about the video you watch, give you feedback, speak to you, and keep your settings and progress. LAMISS does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use or share it for anything unrelated to that purpose.

Who else processes it

  • Google: to sign you in.
  • OpenAI: the video's captions, your questions and answers (as text) and your voice recordings are sent to OpenAI's API to generate questions, answers, feedback, transcriptions and, depending on the setup, speech. LAMISS asks OpenAI not to store the generated responses. According to OpenAI's API data usage policy, OpenAI does not train its models on API data by default and may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse.
  • A text-to-speech server (Kokoro, run for LAMISS on rented cloud GPUs from RunPod): the text LAMISS reads aloud.
  • The hosting provider of LAMISS's server and database. It will be named here before LAMISS is made publicly available.

How long LAMISS keeps your data

Until you delete your account. Nothing is deleted automatically while your account exists.

Deleting your data

Open the LAMISS popup, go to Profile & Account, and choose Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, settings, conversations, answers and progress from LAMISS's server. It cannot be undone. If you sign in again later, you start with a new, empty account. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes the settings kept in your browser.

You can also e-mail sanchezramosdaniel@gmail.com to ask for your account to be deleted, or for a copy or correction of your data. You will get an answer within 30 days.

What is not deleted, because it does not belong to any one user: the videos' captions and the questions generated for a video. Data already sent to OpenAI follows OpenAI's policy above.

Children

LAMISS is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has used LAMISS, write to the address above and the account will be deleted.

Security

The extension only talks to LAMISS's server over encrypted connections (HTTPS and WSS), and your sign-in is kept in the browser's session storage, which is cleared when the browser closes.

Changes

If what LAMISS collects or who processes it changes, this page and its date will be updated, and the extension will ask you to accept the new notice the next time you sign in.
